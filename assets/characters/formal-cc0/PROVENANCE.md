## AG ingestion rules

1. Do not treat the historical `05_q_adult_male.glb` or `06_q_adult_female_repaired.glb` as provenance-proven Quaternius assets. Their origin remains unverified and they stay TEST/HOLD.
2. A new AG formal candidate must be derived from a freshly obtained official free Standard package, not from an unknown/repacked third-party binary.
3. Before a downloaded model can enter the formal candidate manifest, record at minimum: official source URL, retrieval date, exact source filename/path, source archive SHA-256, derived asset SHA-256, role, modifications, and license evidence.
4. Preserve the upstream CC0 evidence with the candidate record. Do not infer license from filenames.
5. New candidates remain TEST-only and `prodEligible:false` until source integrity, runtime geometry, required animation compatibility, AG visual review, and license/provenance gates all PASS.
6. Adult male/female compatibility is verified first. Child roles are created only after the adult route is technically stable; Teen proportions may be evaluated as a child-role base but do not automatically satisfy AG visual requirements.
7. Do not replace existing PROD characters or remove historical test evidence during candidate preparation.
8. Do not use paid Quaternius Pro/Source packages, trials, Mixamo redistribution, or any asset whose permanent commercial/redistribution rights cannot be proven.
